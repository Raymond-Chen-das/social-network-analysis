# Phase 5 — DNA Summary
Single source of truth, generated by `src/dna_analysis.py`.
## Methodology
- **Sliding window**: 6-month window, 3-month step (50% overlap).
- **Date range**: 2015-01-01 to 2024-03-31.
- **Tag pools**: community-specific curated pools (identical to Phase 4 ENA; 97/363/41/384/23 tags).
- **Louvain**: resolution=1.0, random_state=42. Metric: modularity Q.
- **Null model** (ai_ml only): N=5 double_edge_swap rewirings per window.
- **Breakpoint detection**: rolling z-score on first differences of Q (|z|>2, window=6).
- **Tech events**: 7 annotated events from CLAUDE_CODE_PROMPT.md.
